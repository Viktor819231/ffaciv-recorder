FFACiv Recorder 0.7.28 / UI33

- Fixed: a busy PC could stop the recorder from recording a game. When Civ (or anything else) kept the processor very
  busy, the recorder stopped looking at Civ and stopped copying its autosaves, so a whole multiplayer game could be
  missed while the window said "waiting for a game". Now a busy PC only slows the recorder down: autosaves are always
  copied (only the few seconds right after each new turn wait), and the window says "Your PC is busy - the recorder
  holds back" while it does.
- Changed: with uploads "After the game", a finished game now uploads once Civ is closed (no longer as soon as Civ is
  back at its main menu), and the replay goes public 2 hours after that upload.
- Fixed: one game the site refused (for example an expired one) stopped every later game from uploading. That game now
  waits 30 minutes and the other games go on.
- Fixed: a PC sleep in the middle of an upload could stop uploads until the recorder was restarted.
- Fixed: a full disk or a locked file could close the recorder.
- Delete now hides the replay on ffaciv.com at once, also while you are in a game.
- Old games: the first Manual upload asks once whether to send smaller uploads or whole saves. The recorder remembers
  which old saves it has already checked, so starting it no longer reads every autosave on the PC again.
- Lighter: while the window is hidden the recorder no longer rebuilds the whole window every 3 seconds, and finished
  games are checked on the site every 10 minutes instead of every 15 seconds.
- Smaller fixes: dates in English everywhere; open sections and lists stay open; Minimize to tray no longer ends in
  "Still waiting for confirmation"; a save folder that is offline for a moment (OneDrive) no longer makes old saves
  count as new; files are written safely in case of a power cut; KEK saves are left out at once.

Unsigned beta, the same as 0.7.27. Installing over the current recorder keeps settings and recordings.
