FFACiv Recorder 0.7.27 / UI32

- New: Simple setup. The first screen now offers one click that copies Civ's autosaves and uploads them after each
  game, with no memory reading and nothing processed on your PC, then checks Civ's autosave setting. The full
  step-by-step setup is still there under "Choose each setting".
- Fixed: the recorder could stop noticing Civ after the PC slept. If the PC went to sleep at the exact moment the
  recorder was looking at Civ, it counted the whole sleep as one very slow look and then waited hours between looks,
  showing "Waiting for Civ" during a game and not saving anything. Now it never waits more than 30 seconds.
- Much lighter on the CPU: checking whether Civ is open was the recorder's biggest cost, because Windows lists every
  program on the PC each time and the recorder asked several times a second. It now asks once every 2 seconds.
- Fixed: a "Multiplayer lobby" card stayed after Civ was closed. When a game ended back in the lobby and Civ was then
  closed, the recorder kept showing that lobby until it was restarted. It now clears as soon as Civ closes, also while
  the PC is busy.

Unsigned beta, the same as 0.7.26. Installing over the current recorder keeps settings and recordings.
