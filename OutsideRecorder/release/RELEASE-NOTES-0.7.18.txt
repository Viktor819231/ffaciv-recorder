FFACiv Recorder 0.7.18 / UI27

- Sends your games and livestreams to ffaciv.com. An install that a test build pointed at the
  test site (ffaciv-test.pages.dev) is moved to ffaciv.com; your other settings stay.
- Streaming is now its own tab, beside Recorder.
- The tab keeps the Follow me link and the current game or lobby together.
- Start a private stream from the current save without replacing the invite link.
- Mark a recording done from the menu. Short games you choose to keep stay saved.
- After you stop playing, approved uploads can finish. Existing recording choices stay.

Unsigned beta, the same as 0.7.17. Installing over the current recorder keeps settings and recordings.
