FFACiv Recorder 0.7.22 / UI29

- The livestream keeps up: the recorder's own small CPU allowance no longer holds back sending saves.
  Before, packing each turn's save went over the allowance for a few seconds, the recorder stopped the
  upload halfway and started it over later, so a livestream could fall many turns behind and the
  Saves list said "Paused to keep your PC smooth". The recorder already runs at below-normal priority,
  so Civ always gets the processor first.
- Fewer gaps in real-time moves: going over that allowance now only slows reading the game a little
  (2 looks a second) instead of pausing it at every new turn.
- A really busy PC (processor 85 % or more) or low memory still holds back packing and uploads as before.

Unsigned beta, the same as 0.7.21. Installing over the current recorder keeps settings and recordings.
