FFACiv Recorder 0.7.19 / UI28

- Saves list on every recording: which saves are on ffaciv.com, which wait on this PC, and why
  they wait (upload timing, automatic uploads off, PC busy, no connection, livestream not ready).
  A save the site refuses shows the site's own reason in red.
- Once the website update is live, each save also shows what the site made of it: in the
  replay, waiting to be converted, or could not be read.
- Deleting a replay can be undone for 7 days. It is hidden from your list and from the website at
  once, and erased for good after 7 days. Undo is on the message and under Previous games.
- The Recorder tab shows only the two newest recordings. The rest are under Previous games
  (renamed from Games). Deleting one leaves an Undo card in its place; no older game moves up.
- Recordings show the turns they hold, for example "T30–144", instead of a turn count.

Unsigned beta, the same as 0.7.18. Installing over the current recorder keeps settings and recordings.
